Tier 2 AI Model

Continuous Sleep Depth (SDI) & 96-Feature Engine

Transcending 5 discrete steps into continuous neurobiological synchronization

Slide 5 of 9 • 3:15–4:05

SDI Transformer Network (Zhou et al., npj Digital Medicine 2025)

Zero-Shot Transformer

Computes a continuous Sleep Depth Index ($0.0 \to 1.0$) per 30-s epoch. Unlike discrete AASM staging, SDI quantifies gradual micro-arousals, sleep debt dissipation, and cyclic alternating patterns (CAP).

RB SDI < 0.20 Shallow burden
AP Mean SDI Average depth
CV SD / Mean Instability
MDR REM Depth Mean in REM
PR REM Share Model REM %
